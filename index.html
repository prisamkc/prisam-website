About

A bit about my background

I’m Prisam KC, a Civil Engineering student with a growing interest in engineering design, transportation planning, surveying, and geospatial analysis. I enjoy working through practical problems — from how a road intersection handles traffic flow to how survey data turns into an accurate site plan.

Alongside coursework, I’ve been building familiarity with the software civil engineers rely on day to day, including tools for drafting, structural analysis, traffic modelling, and GIS mapping. I’m still early in my journey and focused on strengthening these fundamentals through practice and hands-on project work.
